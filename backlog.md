- [x] **Feature 8**: Middle School Curriculum Expansion (Grades 8 & 9 / כיתות ח׳–ט׳) - Comprehensive mission packs for Grades 8 & 9 (24 missions total, 4 units × 3 missions per grade) strictly aligned with Israeli Ministry of Education requirements: Band II completion (Grade 8: Past Continuous, Present Perfect Simple, comparatives/superlatives, First Conditional) and Band III entry (Grade 9: Passive Voice, Second Conditional, Relative Clauses, Past Perfect) with Boss review missions and deterministic exercise option & word chip shuffling.
- [x] **Feature 16**: Elementary School Curriculum Expansion (Grades 1–6 / כיתות א׳–ו׳) - Comprehensive mission packs for all elementary grades (72 missions total, 4 units × 3 missions per grade) strictly aligned with Israeli Ministry of Education requirements: Pre-Foundation phonics, letter sounds & sight words (Grades 1–2), Pre-Band I early reading (Grade 3), and Band I core vocabulary, action verbs, and basic grammar (Grades 4–6) with picture-emoji vocabulary matching, age-appropriate tier scaling, and deterministic exercise option & word chip shuffling.
- [x] **Feature 17**: High School & Bagrut Curriculum Expansion (Grades 10–12 / כיתות י׳–י״ב) - Comprehensive mission packs for all high school grades (36 missions total, 4 units × 3 missions per grade) strictly aligned with Israeli Ministry of Education requirements: Band III advanced vocabulary, Passive Voice, Relative Clauses, Modals of Deduction in the Present and Past, Conditionals (First, Second, and Third), HOTS literature analysis (Cause & Effect, Distinguishing Perspectives, Comparing & Contrasting), Bagrut Opinion Essay architecture (120–140 words), and rigorous unseen reading preparation (Modules A–E/G).
- [x] **Feature 11**: Diagnostic Level Placement Test (מבחן רמה / אבחון ראשוני) - 10-question adaptive test offered after first-time onboarding (skippable) and on demand from the dashboard. Questions are generated from every mission's grammar-rule and vocabulary drill seeds; an up/down staircase across grades 1–12 estimates the learner's level and recommends a grade and proficiency tier (*Explorer* / *Challenger* / *Master*), with a one-time +30 XP / +10 💎 completion reward and cross-device result sync.
- [x] **Feature 18**: "Get Ready for an Exam" (הכנה למבחן מותאמת אישית) - The learner picks the exam date (up to 21 days ahead), the curriculum units and the skills (Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence building, Unseen reading). The app builds a day-by-day study plan (Shabbat-aware, with catch-up for missed days) or a 1-day crash course: short practice sessions drawn from the units' mission drills, a mixed review day, and a 12-question Mock Mini-Exam (סימולציית מבחן) with a per-skill breakdown and confidence tips. A mock score of 70%+ earns the "Exam Ready" certificate; plans sync across devices.
- [x] **Feature 15**: Star-Gated Boss Missions & Dedicated Missions-Completed Metric (פתיחת משימות בוס בכוכבים ומונה משימות נפרד) - Each unit's Boss mission unlocks only after earning 4 of 6 stars on the unit's regular missions (earlier Boss progress is kept); the mission map and the daily mission card show how many stars are missing and suggest which mission to replay. The dashboard stats strip adds a "Missions Completed" (משימות הושלמו) counter next to total stars (stars = mastery depth, missions = volume). Star milestones for speed challenges and Games Book mini-games will be added with those features (Feature 12).
- [x] **Feature 10**: School Word List Importer (רשימות המילים שלי) - The learner pastes a word list from a school worksheet (words with optional Hebrew translations, or sentences); the app reads it, fills missing translations from the Ministry word bank, and saves up to 10 lists synced across devices. Practice sessions combine a meaning match, letter magnets for spelling, word magnets for sentences and a voice drill, with a once-a-day reward per list.
- [x] **Feature 12**: My English Room (החדר האנגלי שלי) - One cozy room that holds the rewards, games and creative extras; every fun action includes an English action (say, spell, write or translate). Phase 1: a drawn room with fixed slots, an avatar that walks to what you tap, items earned from first-time 3-star missions and shop decor/stickers, placed only after saying or spelling their English names, and free starter avatars. Phase 2: 🚪 Wardrobe Dress-Up Studio (17 clothes & accessories, tier-based naming, daily dress-up reward, shop accessories). Phase 3: 📱 Voice Messages (grade-band replies recorded and scored by voice). Phase 4: 🛏️ Dream Journal (invent a dream in Hebrew with guided frames, translate it to English, read it aloud, saved journal). Phase 5: 📖 Games Book (Word Scramble, Word Search, Crossword and Hidden Words from grade vocabulary and word lists, unlocked at 0/10/25/40 stars). Phase 6: 🎨 Sticker Studio (sticker album for the board and the app background; premium drawing with the Sticker Studio Pass, named in English and saved in the profile). Each activity gives a once-a-day reward that counts toward the daily quests and streak. The per-item missions follow draft designs and can be refined when a detailed breakdown is provided.
- [x] **Feature 20**: Fix Hints (תיקון רמזים) - Audited every drill hint and replaced the ones that repeated the prompt or gave the answer away. Vocabulary hints now give the letter count, an English example sentence or a fill-in-the-blank sentence (by tier); sentence magnets give the first and last words; voice drills give a pronunciation tip for Hebrew speakers; story questions point to the sentence that holds the answer; and all 144 Grammar Detective steps have a hand-written rule hint. Word lists and the room naming panel use the same helpers, and a test sweeps every mission and tier so a hint never repeats the prompt or names the answer.
- [x] **Feature 19**: Friends Hub & Daily Contests (מרכז חברים ותחרויות יומיות) - A social hub for signed-in learners. Friends are added by a 6-character friend code, a username, or an invite link shared through WhatsApp or the native share sheet; a friendship starts only after the invited learner accepts. Friend cards show the avatar, display name, level, streak, stars and missions completed, with preset reactions only (no chat) and remove at any time. Friends see nothing else (no age, grade or email), and a settings toggle turns the hub off. Daily contests: a friend is invited to a same-day contest of 5 meaning matches and 3 sentence magnets. Each player gets questions from their own grade at the same relative difficulty (same unit, tier and layout), with no hints, one try per question and a timer. The higher score wins and the faster time breaks a tie. Finishing counts as a mission; a win gives 10 💎 and taking part gives 3 💎. Friends, public profiles and contests live in new Firestore collections guarded by security rules.
- [x] **Feature 9**: Parent / Teacher Progress Summary & Weekly Report (דוח התקדמות להורים ולמורים) - A dashboard card shows this week's practice days and opens a weekly report (Sunday–Saturday, with arrows back to earlier weeks). The report covers practice days with the Shabbat shield, sessions, new missions completed, stars gained, voice words and XP; overall level, streak, missions completed and a star mastery rating; and the words mastered this week (missions that reached 3 stars). A Hebrew text summary is shared through WhatsApp or the native share sheet, or copied. A new 12-week daily activity log is recorded centrally from every profile update and merged across devices.

The mission victory is now staged over about four seconds and can be skipped with a tap: the stars land one by one (a missed star lands grey), the XP bar fills toward the next level with a level-up badge, the XP, diamonds and words count up, and the 3-star room item flips in last.
