## Completed Features
- [x] **Shared drill session hook and Button** - The mission, exam, word-list and contest runners share `useDrillSession` (`hooks/useDrillSession.ts`, a tested reducer) for their step, answer and feedback state, and `components/ui/Button.tsx` (44px minimum, `sm`/`md`/`lg`, primary/secondary/danger) replaces the hand-rolled text buttons across the app. Icon-only buttons, toggles with a conditional variant and the grade/theme pickers keep their own markup.
- [x] **Feature 47**: Hebrew audio for young learners (הקראה בעברית) - Fixed Hebrew instructions, hints and feedback are read by a Hebrew voice (Azure `en-US-AvaMultilingualNeural` speaking Hebrew, switching to native English for English words), rendered as clips for grades 1–2 and through the online voice otherwise. A 🔊 sits beside every instruction, hint, feedback message and Hebrew question card, and every answer option has its own listen-only 🔊. Grades 1–2 hear the instruction and then the English question as each step opens, the hint when it opens, and the answer then its feedback. The placement test opens by asking "אפשר כבר לקרוא באנגלית?" (read aloud); "עוד לא" skips the questions and recommends grade 1 from the letters.
- [x] **Feature 48**: Reading from zero in grade 1 (קריאה מאפס) - Grade 1 now has 10 units: the letters A–Z (units 1–4: each letter's name, capital and small form and three picture words; hear and pick, small to capital, picture to first letter, say the word, letter to picture), blending (units 5–6: words as tiles that slide together, reading alone, building the word with letter magnets, look-alike words one letter apart, sh / ch / th, sight words and a first sentence), then the earlier topics as units 7–10. Mini-lessons show a card per letter and sliding word tiles. Existing stars stay on their missions (the new units have new ids).
- [x] **Security hardening: App Check and API key** - Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA Enterprise) is enforced for Firestore (client start in `services/firebase.ts` via `VITE_APPCHECK_SITE_KEY`) and for `/api/tts` (`APPCHECK_ENFORCE=on`, `api/_lib/appCheckToken.ts`); Authentication stays in monitor mode. The web app uses its own new browser key, restricted to the production, firebaseapp and localhost sites and to the Firebase APIs it needs. With the earlier `firestore.rules` and the enforced CSP, this closes the hardening item.
- [x] **Logout from the profile screen** (התנתקות מהפרופיל) - "התנתקות מהחשבון" is now in the profile settings, in the "פרטיות וחשבון" section (a guest is asked to confirm first, since an unsaved guest account is deleted). The cloud button in the home ribbon shows only when something needs attention: a sync problem or an unsaved guest account.
- [x] **Feature 46**: Question screen hierarchy (מסך שאלה ברור) - The exercise itself is the big, central card in missions, exam prep, word lists and contests: the Hebrew sentence to build, the Hebrew word to put into English, the English word to explain, or the question with its blank drawn as a slot. One short instruction above it, the step badge small, and the hint in a popover from the 💡 button (only when tapped). The voice studio shows the sentence large with its Hebrew translation, slow and normal listening in the card, and one round record button. - [x] **Feature 45**: App tour (סיור באפליקציה) - A short, colourful slideshow led by the theme's companion: welcome, missions and their 5 steps, the map with stars and the Boss castle, XP and diamonds, the streak and daily quests, the dock (room, exam prep, word lists, friends, report) and a finish slide. - [x] **Feature 43**: New exam prep questions (שאלות חדשות להכנה למבחן) - "Get Ready for an Exam" has its own question bank for every grade instead of reusing the mission questions, so no question appears in two places. - [x] **Feature 19**: Friends Hub & Daily Contests (מרכז חברים ותחרויות יומיות) - A social hub for signed-in learners. Friends are added by a 6-character friend code, a username, or an invite link shared through WhatsApp or the native share sheet; a friendship starts only after the invited learner accepts. Friend cards show the avatar, display name, level, streak, stars and missions completed, with preset reactions only (no chat) and remove at any time. Friends see nothing else (no age, grade or email), and a settings toggle turns the hub off. Daily contests: a friend is invited to a same-day contest of 5 meaning matches and 3 sentence magnets. Each player gets questions from their own grade at the same relative difficulty (same unit, tier and layout), with no hints, one try per question and a timer. The higher score wins and the faster time breaks a tie. Finishing counts as a mission; a win gives 10 💎 and taking part gives 3 💎. Friends, public profiles and contests live in new Firestore collections guarded by security rules.
