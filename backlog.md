# English Tutor App - Product Backlog

## Future / Next Up Ideas
*Sorted by significance to the app's development (highest priority first). Feature numbers are stable IDs, not priority.*

- [ ] **Feature 19**: Friends Hub & Daily Contests (מרכז חברים ותחרויות יומיות) - A social hub where learners connect with friends who already use the app, compare progress, and challenge each other to short English contests that reward diamonds.
  1. **Adding Friends (הוספת חברים)**: Invite existing app users from inside the app (by username or friend code), or share an invite link through a messaging app (e.g., WhatsApp) using the device's native share sheet. A friendship is created only after the invited friend accepts.
  2. **Friends Stats (סטטיסטיקות חברים)**: Each friend card shows avatar, display name, level, current streak, total stars and missions completed (Feature 15 counter).
  3. **Daily Contest Challenge (אתגר יומי)**: Invite a friend to a contest, a short English mini-game that both must complete today (before midnight). Both players get the same challenge, built with the existing deterministic seeded shuffle so options appear in the same order for both. The winner is decided by accuracy score, with completion time as the tie-breaker. The winner earns diamonds; a small participation reward keeps it friendly for the other player. Contests use existing drill types (vocabulary match, sentence builder, voice drill) until the Games Book mini-games (Feature 12) exist.
  4. **Kid-Safety Guardrails (בטיחות ילדים)**: No free-text chat between users, only preset reactions/emojis. Profiles show only display name and avatar (no email, age or school). Friends can be removed at any time, and a parent can turn the hub off.
- [ ] **Feature 9**: Parent / Teacher Progress Summary Card & Exportable Weekly Report (tracking missions completed count, star mastery rating, weekly streaks, and mastered vocabulary).
- [ ] **Feature 12**: My English Room — Interactive Mission Room, Customizable Avatar & Sticker Studio (החדר האנגלי שלי: חדר משימות, אוואטר ומדבקות) *(merges former Features 12 Stickers Album, 13 Mini-Games Arcade & 14 Room Decorator)* - One cozy room that serves as the home for all rewards, games and creative extras, instead of separate screens. Guiding rule: every fun action in the room includes an English action (say, spell, write or translate), so the room stays a place to practice English.
  1. **The Avatar (אוואטר)**: A person or one of several animals, living in the room. Learners move the avatar onto an object to start that object's activity (e.g., put the avatar in bed to start the bed mission).
  2. **Room Items**, in three kinds:
     - **Mission items (פריטי משימות)**, each opening a distinct English skill drill:
       - 🛏️ **Bed (מיטה)** — *Dream Journal* (writing & translation): invent a dream, write it in Hebrew, then try to translate it into English.
       - 📱 **Phone on the desk (טלפון)** — *Voice Messages* (speaking): record English voice messages, scored with the existing speech-similarity engine.
       - 🚪 **Wardrobe (ארון)** — *Dress-Up Studio* (vocabulary): dress and style the avatar (clothes for humans, accessories such as hats and glasses for every avatar). A clothing item or accessory is put on by saying or spelling its English name.
     - **Game items (פריטי משחק)**:
       - 📖 **Games Book on the floor (ספר משחקים)** — opens all word games (crosswords, Word Scramble, Word Search תפזורת, word finding). Games unlock progressively through star milestones (Feature 15).
     - **Decorative items (פריטי נוי)** — e.g., the 💡 small desk lamp, sofa, plants and shelves, purely for the room's look.
  3. **Earning & Placing Items** (from former Feature 14): completing a mission with 3 stars awards a furniture or decor item; Reward Shop decor and stickers (Feature 5) also land here. To place any item in the room, the learner must first say or spell its English name correctly (e.g., "sofa"), which reinforces active vocabulary recall.
  4. **Sticker Studio (יצירת מדבקות)** (from former Feature 12, extended): an album of collected stickers that learners can use to decorate the room and the app background. **Premium (פרימיום):** learners can draw their own stickers and then use them; a newly drawn sticker is saved once the learner gives it an English name (typed or spoken).
  5. **Pending**: A detailed breakdown of the missions for each room item will be provided later.
  - **Progress**: ✅ Phase 1 done — the room, avatar walking, earning items from 3-star missions and shop decor/stickers, placing items by saying or spelling their names, and free starter avatars. Next phases: 🚪 Wardrobe Dress-Up, 📱 Voice Messages, 🛏️ Dream Journal, 📖 Games Book, Sticker Studio.

- [ ] **Feature 20**: Fix Hints (תיקון רמזים) - Some hints repeat the question itself, so they give no real help. Audit the hints in every mission and drill, and rewrite any hint that just restates the prompt so it gives a useful clue without revealing the answer.
- [ ] **Feature 21**: App Name Change (שינוי שם האפליקציה) - The app name is still TBD. Choose the new name and update every place it appears in the UI, page titles, manifest and docs.
- [ ] **Feature 22**: UI Audit & Game-Like Redesign Proposal (ביקורת ממשק והצעה לעיצוב משחקי) - Audit the current UI (dashboard, mission map, mission runner, modals and rewards) and propose drastic improvements toward a more graphical, game-like experience (bolder illustrations, animated characters, world-style progress, richer reward feedback). Deliver the audit and a written proposal before any implementation.

- [x] **Feature 7**: Vercel Cloud Hosting & Production Environment Setup.
- [x] **Feature 8**: Middle School Curriculum Expansion (Grades 8 & 9 / כיתות ח׳–ט׳) - Comprehensive mission packs for Grades 8 & 9 (24 missions total, 4 units × 3 missions per grade) strictly aligned with Israeli Ministry of Education requirements: Band II completion (Grade 8: Past Continuous, Present Perfect Simple, comparatives/superlatives, First Conditional) and Band III entry (Grade 9: Passive Voice, Second Conditional, Relative Clauses, Past Perfect) with Boss review missions and deterministic exercise option & word chip shuffling.
- [x] **Feature 16**: Elementary School Curriculum Expansion (Grades 1–6 / כיתות א׳–ו׳) - Comprehensive mission packs for all elementary grades (72 missions total, 4 units × 3 missions per grade) strictly aligned with Israeli Ministry of Education requirements: Pre-Foundation phonics, letter sounds & sight words (Grades 1–2), Pre-Band I early reading (Grade 3), and Band I core vocabulary, action verbs, and basic grammar (Grades 4–6) with picture-emoji vocabulary matching, age-appropriate tier scaling, and deterministic exercise option & word chip shuffling.
- [x] **Feature 17**: High School & Bagrut Curriculum Expansion (Grades 10–12 / כיתות י׳–י״ב) - Comprehensive mission packs for all high school grades (36 missions total, 4 units × 3 missions per grade) strictly aligned with Israeli Ministry of Education requirements: Band III advanced vocabulary, Passive Voice, Relative Clauses, Modals of Deduction in the Present and Past, Conditionals (First, Second, and Third), HOTS literature analysis (Cause & Effect, Distinguishing Perspectives, Comparing & Contrasting), Bagrut Opinion Essay architecture (120–140 words), and rigorous unseen reading preparation (Modules A–E/G).
- [x] **Feature 11**: Diagnostic Level Placement Test (מבחן רמה / אבחון ראשוני) - 10-question adaptive test offered after first-time onboarding (skippable) and on demand from the dashboard. Questions are generated from every mission's grammar-rule and vocabulary drill seeds; an up/down staircase across grades 1–12 estimates the learner's level and recommends a grade and proficiency tier (*Explorer* / *Challenger* / *Master*), with a one-time +30 XP / +10 💎 completion reward and cross-device result sync.
- [x] **Feature 18**: "Get Ready for an Exam" (הכנה למבחן מותאמת אישית) - The learner picks the exam date (up to 21 days ahead), the curriculum units and the skills (Vocabulary, Grammar, Sentence building, Unseen reading). The app builds a day-by-day study plan (Shabbat-aware, with catch-up for missed days) or a 1-day crash course: short practice sessions drawn from the units' mission drills, a mixed review day, and a 12-question Mock Mini-Exam (סימולציית מבחן) with a per-skill breakdown and confidence tips. A mock score of 70%+ earns the "Exam Ready" certificate; plans sync across devices.
- [x] **Feature 15**: Star-Gated Boss Missions & Dedicated Missions-Completed Metric (פתיחת משימות בוס בכוכבים ומונה משימות נפרד) - Each unit's Boss mission unlocks only after earning 4 of 6 stars on the unit's regular missions (earlier Boss progress is kept); the mission map and the daily mission card show how many stars are missing and suggest which mission to replay. The dashboard stats strip adds a "Missions Completed" (משימות הושלמו) counter next to total stars (stars = mastery depth, missions = volume). Star milestones for speed challenges and Games Book mini-games will be added with those features (Feature 12).
- [x] **Feature 10**: School Word List Importer (רשימות המילים שלי) - The learner pastes a word list from a school worksheet (words with optional Hebrew translations, or sentences); the app reads it, fills missing translations from the Ministry word bank, and saves up to 10 lists synced across devices. Practice sessions combine a meaning match, letter magnets for spelling, word magnets for sentences and a voice drill, with a once-a-day reward per list.
